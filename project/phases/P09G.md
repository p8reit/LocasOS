## Goal and acceptance

Audit current runtime authentication and security-sensitive HTTP operations, privileged app/update IPC and root workers. Generate server-owned correlation and independent noncredential session references. Carry typed attribution into queues; distinguish administrator assertions, Agent-observed peer UID and root writer UID. Persist private bounded hash-linked journals; sync intent before actions and result before normal success. Expose uncertainty when effects and logging cannot be atomic. Exclude credentials, tokens, prompts/replies, bodies, arbitrary paths and raw errors.

Acceptance includes journal integrity, schema, bounds, permissions, restart/multiprocess and failure tests; spoof rejection and redaction; intent failure blocks effects; outcome failure suppresses cookies/success; durable worker checkpoints; packaging/private directory preservation; a read-only verifier; local checks, independent review and matching PR CI.

## Design and files

See [audit contract and recovery](../../docs/security/auditing.md), [IPC contract](../../docs/architecture/agent-contract.md) and [threat model](../../docs/security/threat-model.md). Implementation: `internal/auditlog`, `internal/auditop`, `internal/admin`, `internal/peercred`, `internal/appmanager`, `internal/updateapproval`, `internal/updatequeue`; `cmd/locas-{core,agent,updater,app-worker,audit}`; WSL/native builders, units, archive allowlists and CI.

Each service has its own 16MiB journal, 4096-byte record maximum and 4096-byte reservation per pending result. No silent eviction/rotation/repair. HTTP output is bounded at8MiB and withheld until result persistence; IPC responses are bounded at8192bytes before acceptance. Root logs queue principal/peer as assertions, stamps its own observed writer UID. Successful ordinary polling reads are excluded. HEAD discovery is audited. Outcome failures, handler panics, partial installer failures and ambiguous mutation IPC produce uncertainty rather than proof of no effect.

## Risks, guardrails and next actions

Hash chains detect retained corruption, not full rewrite/deletion/valid-prefix truncation or writer/root compromise without external anchors. Local clock is not independently trusted. Full journals intentionally deny new audited work; authorized evidence-preserving retention/recovery remains manual. Pending/uncertain effects require reconciliation; automatic replay is excluded. Manual install/repair scripts remain outside this runtime guarantee. Legacy plaintext/unattributed queued jobs fail closed; upgrade all cooperating binaries/units together and request fresh approval after reconciliation.
